CYBER AWARENESS

Stay Safe in the Digital World

Cybersecurity awareness means understanding common online risks and knowing how to protect yourself.

🔑

Protect Your Passwords

Use strong and unique passwords for important accounts.

📱

Secure Your Devices

Keep your phone and computer updated with the latest security updates.

🧠

Think Before Clicking

Always check suspicious links, messages and unexpected attachments.

COMMON THREATS

Know the Risks

Understanding common cyber threats is the first step towards staying safe online.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites designed to trick people into sharing sensitive information.

🦠

Malware

Malicious software that can damage devices or compromise personal information.

💳

Online Fraud

Scams that attempt to steal money or sensitive information through online platforms.

🪪

Identity Theft

Unauthorized use of someone's personal information for fraudulent purposes.

SAFETY TIPS

Simple Habits, Better Protection

Follow these basic cybersecurity practices in your everyday digital life.

🔐

Strong Passwords

Create long and unique passwords for different accounts.

🛡️

Enable 2FA

Use two-factor authentication whenever it is available.

🔗

Check Links

Verify links before entering passwords or personal information.

🔄

Update Devices

Keep your operating system and applications updated.

👤

Protect Privacy

Avoid sharing unnecessary personal information online.

🚫

Never Share OTP

Never share OTPs, passwords or verification codes with anyone.
