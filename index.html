KNOW THE RISKS

Common Cyber Threats

Understanding threats helps you recognize them before they cause harm.

01

Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information.

02

Malware

Malicious software can damage devices or steal information.

03

Online Fraud

Scammers may use fake offers, messages or websites to steal money or personal information.

04

Identity Theft

Criminals may misuse personal information to impersonate someone.

STAY PROTECTED

Essential Safety Tips

Follow these simple rules to improve your online security.

01

Never Share OTPs

Never share your OTP or verification code with anyone.

02

Keep Software Updated

Install security updates and keep your apps and operating system updated.

03

Check Links Before Clicking

Be careful with unexpected links and suspicious websites.

04

Protect Your Privacy

Avoid sharing unnecessary personal information online.
