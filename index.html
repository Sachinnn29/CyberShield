ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why cybersecurity awareness matters

Students use smartphones, social media, email, online payments and educational platforms every day. Understanding basic cybersecurity can help prevent avoidable mistakes.

01

Objective

Understand students' awareness of common cybersecurity risks and promote safer online behaviour.

02

Topics Covered

Phishing, malware, online fraud, identity theft, passwords, privacy, OTP safety and 2FA.

03

Student Survey

Students participated in a cybersecurity awareness survey to share their online safety habits.

KNOW THE RISKS

Common Cyber Threats

Knowing common threats makes it easier to recognise suspicious activity.

01

Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites designed to steal passwords, OTPs or personal information.

Watch for suspicious links and urgent messages.
02

Malware

Malicious software that can affect devices or expose information.

Avoid unknown files and unsafe downloads.
03

Online Fraud

Fake offers, websites and messages used to obtain money or personal information.

Be careful with unrealistic offers.
04

Identity Theft

Personal information may be misused to impersonate someone or access accounts.

Monitor unusual account activity.

PRACTICAL HABITS

Simple Safety Tips

Small habits can make a big difference in online safety.

01

Strong Passwords

Use strong and unique passwords for important accounts.

02

Enable 2FA

Add two-factor authentication whenever available.

03

Check Links

Think before opening unexpected links or attachments.

04

Update Devices

Keep your operating system and applications updated.

05

Protect Privacy

Avoid sharing unnecessary personal information online.

06

Never Share OTP

Never disclose OTPs or passwords to anyone.
